Research Objective

To systematically review existing research on AI-based automated medical coding, with particular emphasis on:

  • Datasets and benchmarks for medical coding
  • Coding-system coverage (ICD-10-CM/PCS, CPT, HCPCS)
  • LLM and multi-agent approaches for automated coding
  • Publicly accessible resources for CPT and HCPCS coding

Research Questions

RQ1 — Dataset Landscape

What publicly available datasets exist for automated medical coding? Which coding systems do they cover?

RQ2 — AI Methods

What AI-based approaches have been proposed? What datasets and coding systems evaluate them?

RQ3 — Multi-Agent Systems

How have multi-agent systems been applied to automated medical coding?

RQ4 — CPT/HCPCS Gap

What gaps exist in publicly accessible datasets for automated CPT and HCPCS coding?

Publication Period

January 1, 2020 — September 18, 2026

Rationale

  • Captures recent advances in LLM-based and agentic approaches
  • Pre-2020 studies belong to the pre-LLM era
  • Focus on current state-of-the-art methods

Inclusion Criteria

  • IC1: Published between January 1, 2020 and September 18, 2026
  • IC2: Published in peer-reviewed journals and conferences
  • IC3: Focus on automated medical coding using AI/ML/NLP/LLM
  • IC4: Report on ICD-10-CM, ICD-10-PCS, CPT, or HCPCS coding systems
  • IC5: Describe or evaluate datasets, benchmarks, or resources
  • IC6: Propose or evaluate multi-agent or agentic approaches
  • IC7: Discuss explainability, verification, validation, or uncertainty
  • IC8: Available in full text

Exclusion Criteria

  • EC1: Published before January 1, 2020
  • EC2: Review articles, editorials, commentaries, book chapters
  • EC3: Not focused on medical coding (e.g., general clinical NLP)
  • EC4: Focus only on ICD-9 (unless comparative with ICD-10)
  • EC5: No empirical results (purely theoretical papers)
  • EC6: Duplicate publications
  • EC7: Preprints without peer review
